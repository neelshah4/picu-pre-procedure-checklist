## Preamble

Prior to all procedures, a check list reviewing the procedural plan should be formulated by
the fellow and reviewed with the supervising faculty (attached to this email). A laminated
copy of this checklist will also be available on the line carts in the unit and in the
workrooms.

## Intubations

Faculty supervision is expected of all intubations. The degree of supervision may vary from
present at the bedside (expected for all first-year fellows) to actively present near the
bedspace at the discretion of the faculty and fellow.

## Central Venous Lines

All central venous lines placed by a first-year fellow in the first 6 months of the academic
year should be directly supervised by a faculty member (+/- supervisory fellow).

When a procedure is being completed by a first-year fellow with the supervision of a senior
fellow, faculty are expected to supervise in a way that feedback can be provided to both the
first-year fellow and supervising fellow.

All central venous lines placed by a senior fellow should be placed with faculty support
immediately available.

## Chest Tubes and Central Arterial Lines

Direct bedside faculty supervision is expected of all chest tube and central arterial line
placement.

## Peripheral Arterial Lines, Lumbar Puncture

Supervision of a peripheral arterial line or lumbar puncture is at the discretion of the
faculty and fellow based on clinical scenario and procedural experience.

## Natural Airway Sedation

Faculty bedside presence is required for all natural airway sedations.

## Closing

At the conclusion of all procedures, please fill out the PICU Fellow Brief Feedback Form to
aid our assessment of procedural competence:
